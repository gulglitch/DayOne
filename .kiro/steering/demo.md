# Day One - Video Demo Guide

## Demo Requirements
- **Maximum Length**: 3 minutes (strict)
- **Minimum Content**: At least ONE complete end-to-end user workflow
- **Quality**: Must be clear, audible, and viewable by judges
- **Format**: Standard video formats (MP4, MOV, WebM recommended)

## Recommended Demo Structure (3 minutes)

### Opening (15 seconds)
**Script Template:**
> "Day One simulates a C-suite boardroom to validate startup ideas. Watch six specialized AI agents debate, challenge, and refine this concept in real-time."

**Visuals:**
- Show landing page hero section
- Quick pan across agent marquee showing all 6 agents

### Problem & Solution (20 seconds)
**Script Template:**
> "Startup validation is expensive and slow. Founders waste months and money before getting structured feedback. Day One provides instant, adversarial analysis from Research, Product, Finance, Legal, Marketing, and CEO agents."

**Visuals:**
- Show problem statement text overlay
- Quick highlight of target users (founders, entrepreneurs)

### Live Workflow Demo (2 minutes)
**This is the critical section - must show complete workflow**

#### Step 1: Idea Submission (15 seconds)
**Action:**
- Navigate to homepage
- Enter startup idea in the input field
- Example idea: "AI-powered meal planning app that uses your grocery receipts to suggest recipes"
- Click submit/analyze button

**Script:**
> "Let's validate a real idea: an AI meal planner that uses grocery receipts. I'll submit it and watch the boardroom debate unfold."

#### Step 2: Real-Time Agent Debate (75 seconds)
**Action:**
- Show the live boardroom view with WebSocket streaming
- Let agents run and display their messages in real-time
- Highlight specific agent contributions:
  - Research agent analyzing market
  - Finance agent challenging revenue model
  - Legal agent flagging data privacy concerns
  - CEO agent resolving conflicts

**Script:**
> "The Research agent starts by analyzing competitors. Finance immediately challenges the monetization. Legal flags GDPR concerns for grocery data. Watch the CEO step in to resolve disagreements. This isn't consensus—it's adversarial validation."

**Key Visual Moments:**
- Show agent avatars/names as they speak
- Highlight the "thinking" animation
- Show message streaming character-by-character
- Capture a moment where agents disagree
- Show CEO resolving the conflict

#### Step 3: Final Dossier (30 seconds)
**Action:**
- Show transition to completed dossier
- Scroll through key sections:
  - Executive Summary
  - Market Analysis
  - MVP Definition
  - Revenue Model
  - Legal Structure
  - Marketing Strategy
  - Elevator Pitch

**Script:**
> "In minutes, we get a comprehensive company dossier: validated MVP scope, revenue model, legal structure, go-to-market strategy, and an investor-ready elevator pitch. What used to take weeks of research now takes a coffee break."

### Closing & Call-to-Action (15 seconds)
**Script:**
> "Day One: Turn rough ideas into validated company plans through AI adversarial collaboration. Built with FastAPI, Next.js, and OpenAI—delivering real-time startup validation at scale."

**Visuals:**
- Show project logo/branding
- Display tech stack badges (FastAPI, Next.js, OpenAI, WebSockets)
- End card with project URL (if deployed)

## Technical Recording Tips

### Screen Recording Tools
**Recommended:**
- **OBS Studio** (Free, professional-grade)
- **Loom** (Easy to use, browser-based)
- **QuickTime** (Mac built-in, simple)
- **ShareX** (Windows, free)

### Recording Settings
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD minimum)
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps or 60fps
- **Audio**: Use external microphone if possible (not laptop mic)
- **Browser Zoom**: Set to 100% (avoid zoomed in/out views)

### Pre-Recording Checklist
- [ ] Close unnecessary browser tabs
- [ ] Hide bookmarks bar
- [ ] Disable notifications (Do Not Disturb mode)
- [ ] Clear browser console/dev tools
- [ ] Test audio levels
- [ ] Have example startup idea ready
- [ ] Ensure backend and frontend are running
- [ ] Test the full workflow once before recording

### During Recording
- **Speak clearly and at moderate pace**
- **Avoid dead air** - keep narrating
- **If you make a mistake**, just restart (don't edit mid-recording)
- **Show, don't tell** - let the app speak for itself
- **Highlight key moments** with mouse cursor or visual emphasis

### Audio Tips
- Record in a quiet room
- Use a quality microphone if available
- Avoid background noise (fans, traffic, keyboard clicks)
- Maintain consistent volume
- Add subtle background music (optional, must not overpower voice)

### Visual Dos and Don'ts

**DO:**
✅ Show the full workflow from start to finish
✅ Highlight real-time streaming (this is unique)
✅ Show agent disagreement/conflict resolution
✅ Use a clean, professional example idea
✅ Show the final output quality

**DON'T:**
❌ Rush through sections
❌ Use offensive or inappropriate example ideas
❌ Show errors or broken functionality
❌ Talk too fast (remember: 3 minutes is plenty)
❌ Forget to show the end result

## Example Ideas to Demonstrate

### Good Demo Ideas (Choose One)
1. **AI-powered meal planning from grocery receipts**
   - Clear problem, relatable use case
   - Shows data privacy concerns (Legal agent engagement)
   - Obvious monetization questions (Finance agent engagement)

2. **Student marketplace for textbook swaps**
   - Simple concept, easy to understand
   - Shows market sizing (Research agent)
   - Revenue model debates (Finance vs Product)

3. **AI fitness coach for home workouts**
   - Consumer app that judges can relate to
   - Health/liability concerns (Legal agent)
   - Competitive market (Research agent)

### Avoid These Demo Ideas
❌ Too complex (blockchain, crypto, technical infrastructure)
❌ Niche B2B SaaS that requires domain knowledge
❌ Anything controversial or potentially offensive
❌ Vague concepts without clear user pain points

## Post-Production (Optional)

### Basic Editing
- **Trim**: Remove dead air at start/end
- **Captions**: Add subtitles for accessibility (optional but recommended)
- **Annotations**: Add text overlays to highlight key moments
- **Music**: Subtle background track (ensure royalty-free)

### Tools for Editing
- **Free**: DaVinci Resolve, Shotcut, iMovie
- **Paid**: Adobe Premiere, Final Cut Pro, Camtasia
- **Online**: Kapwing, Clipchamp, Descript

### Accessibility
- Consider adding captions/subtitles
- Ensure text is readable at all sizes
- Use high contrast for any overlays
- Test video on different devices

## Quality Checklist

Before submitting your video, verify:
- [ ] Video is under 3 minutes
- [ ] Shows complete end-to-end workflow
- [ ] Audio is clear and audible
- [ ] Video is high resolution (1080p minimum)
- [ ] No errors or broken functionality shown
- [ ] Highlights real-time streaming feature
- [ ] Shows agent debate/disagreement
- [ ] Displays final dossier output
- [ ] Explains problem and value proposition
- [ ] Link works in incognito/private mode
- [ ] Privacy settings allow judge access

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Content Mistakes
- ❌ Spending too long on setup/intro
- ❌ Not showing the complete workflow
- ❌ Talking about features instead of showing them
- ❌ Using technical jargon without explanation
- ❌ Forgetting to show the end result

### Technical Mistakes
- ❌ Poor audio quality
- ❌ Low video resolution
- ❌ Video link requires login/password
- ❌ Video is private or unlisted incorrectly
- ❌ Recording includes personal information

### Presentation Mistakes
- ❌ Speaking too fast or too slow
- ❌ No energy or enthusiasm in voice
- ❌ Reading from a script robotically
- ❌ Not explaining what's happening on screen
- ❌ Assuming judges know your app already

## Backup Plan

Have a backup recording ready in case:
- Technical issues occur during main demo
- Agent responses are unusually slow
- WebSocket connection drops
- Any unexpected errors

**Recommendation**: Record 2-3 takes and pick the best one.

## Timeline for Demo Creation

**Day 1 (4 hours):**
- Script writing and rehearsal (1 hour)
- Set up recording environment (30 min)
- Practice full workflow 3x (1 hour)
- Record 3-5 takes (1.5 hours)

**Day 2 (2 hours):**
- Select best take
- Basic editing (trim, captions)
- Upload to hosting platform
- Test link and submit

**Total Time Investment**: ~6 hours for a polished demo
