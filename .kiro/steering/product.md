# Day One - Product Overview

Day One is an AI-powered startup validation platform that simulates a C-suite boardroom debate for any startup idea. Six specialized AI agents analyze, challenge, and refine ideas in real-time.

## Core Value Proposition

Transform rough startup ideas into validated company dossiers through adversarial AI collaboration. Watch Research, Product, Finance, Legal, Marketing, and CEO agents debate and produce comprehensive analysis with real-time streaming updates.

## Key Features

- **6 Specialized AI Agents**: Each with distinct roles and personalities
  - Research: Market analysis and competitor research
  - Product: MVP definition and tech stack recommendations
  - Finance: Revenue modeling and financial challenges
  - Legal: Compliance and legal structure guidance
  - Marketing: Go-to-market strategy development
  - CEO: Final decision-making and conflict resolution

- **Adversarial Validation**: Finance and Legal agents actively challenge proposals; CEO resolves conflicts

- **Real-Time Experience**: WebSocket streaming shows agent "thinking" and debates live

- **Comprehensive Output**: Generates full company dossier including MVP scope, revenue model, legal structure, marketing strategy, and elevator pitch

## Target Users

Aspiring founders, startup accelerators, and entrepreneurs seeking rapid validation and structured analysis of business ideas.

## Current Status

Production-ready backend with all 6 agents implemented. Frontend provides landing page, live boardroom view, and final dossier presentation.
